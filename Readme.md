### 📄 Featured Research

**Electrothermal and RF Analysis of Al₀.₃₀Ga₀.₇₀N/GaN HEMTs on 4H-SiC Substrate**
*Satya Prakash, Sayan Mandal, Anjali Kumari, Anant Kumar Kushwaha, Santashraya Prasad*

TCAD-based (SILVACO Atlas) study of thermal resistance (R<sub>TH</sub>) and RF performance (f<sub>T</sub>, f<sub>MAX</sub>) in AlGaN/GaN HEMTs, comparing gate lengths (L<sub>G</sub> = 0.3/0.5 µm) and gate-connected field-plate lengths (L<sub>FP</sub> = 0.4/0.8 µm).

- R<sub>TH</sub> extracted from ΔT vs. P<sub>D</sub> characteristics — 11.40 to 36.19 K/W for L<sub>G</sub> = 0.3 µm
- Devices without a field-plate show a single fixed hotspot/R<sub>TH</sub> at the drain-side gate edge; field-plate devices show **three distinct R<sub>TH</sub> regions** as the hotspot shifts with bias
- R<sub>TH</sub> decreases with increasing L<sub>FP</sub> and L<sub>G</sub>, while C<sub>gs</sub>/C<sub>gd</sub> increase — a direct thermal-vs-RF-speed trade-off
- For L<sub>G</sub> = 0.3 µm without FP: f<sub>T</sub> = 196 GHz, f<sub>MAX</sub> = 284 GHz
- **Conclusion:** gate length and field-plate length can't be optimized independently — both must be co-selected based on whether the design priority is thermal management or RF speed
