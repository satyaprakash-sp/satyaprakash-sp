### 🔬 Research Experience

**Electrothermal & RF Analysis of AlGaN/GaN HEMTs on 4H-SiC Substrate**
*BIT Mesra / BIT Deoghar / Ramgarh Engineering College — collaborative research*

Investigated the combined effect of gate length (L_G) and gate-connected field-plate length (L_FP) on thermal resistance and RF performance of Al₀.₃₀Ga₀.₇₀N/GaN HEMTs, using coupled electrical–thermal TCAD simulation in SILVACO.

**Research Focus**
- Device Self-Heating & Channel Temperature Rise
- Thermal Resistance (R_TH) Extraction from ΔT vs. P_D
- Field-Plate Influence on Hotspot Location
- RF Figures of Merit: f_T and f_MAX
- Gate-Length / Field-Plate Length Trade-off Analysis

**Key Contributions**
- Simulated Al₀.₃₀Ga₀.₇₀N/GaN HEMT structure on 4H-SiC substrate across L_G = 0.3/0.5 µm and L_FP = 0.4/0.8 µm
- Extracted R_TH from linear ΔT vs. P_D regions: 11.40–36.19 K/W range for L_G = 0.3 µm
- Showed devices without FP have a single, fixed hotspot/R_TH at the drain-side gate edge, while GC-FP devices exhibit **three distinct R_TH regions** as the hotspot shifts with bias
- Demonstrated R_TH decreases with increasing L_FP and L_G, while C_gs/C_gd increase — establishing a thermal-vs-RF-speed design trade-off
- Obtained f_T = 196 GHz and f_MAX = 284 GHz for L_G = 0.3 µm without FP

**Technologies:** SILVACO Atlas · TCAD · Drift-Diffusion & Heat-Flow Simulation · Albrecht Mobility Model
